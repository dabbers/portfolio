dabbit is an email and productivity client built around one question: what in here actually needs me? Mail is grouped into contexts rather than folders, and every context lands in one of three views: **Needs you**, **Tracking**, or **Later**. The idea is that the inbox stops being a list of everything and becomes a list of things to do.

Sources can be a live mailbox or an imported archive, so the old stuff gets the same treatment as the new.

The name is recycled from [my old IRC client](/projects/dabb-it). Some names are too good to retire.
