Punatorium.com is a website I built to keep track of puns I've enjoyed. It's more of a creative outlet than a technical demonstration. There's artwork, contracted out since I'm not artsy, that goes on pun-themed T-shirts you can buy.

The site has been going for quite a while. I've long thought it could become more of a technical project: tag up the jokes and feed them to a model to teach it how to play on words that sound or spell the same in different contexts.
