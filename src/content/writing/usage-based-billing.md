title: Usage-based billing is having a moment
description: Consumption pricing puts you on the opposite side of the table from your customer. And the AI labs people point to aren't actually doing it.
date: 2026-11-04
---

Usage-based billing is having a moment, but I'm not convinced it's right for most SaaS.

When revenue scales with consumption, your growth depends on customers using more of the product, whether or not the extra usage provides additional value. Making the product more efficient takes money off your own top line. You've put yourself on the opposite side of the table from the person paying you, every month.

Everyone points at Anthropic and OpenAI as the proof of usage-based billing. Their APIs are usage-based, but that's not what they lead with. The core product is a subscription, priced per seat, with a meter behind it that enforces a limit.

Those two things get called the same thing, and they aren't. Same telemetry, completely different job. One decides whether you're allowed to keep going. The other decides what you owe.
