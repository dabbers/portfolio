My first monetization engineering assignment happened because I was the least busy person on the team. It wasn't part of any career plan. Just "you've got bandwidth, take this."

I didn't expect it to change how I see engineering problems, but it did. Code that works isn't enough on its own. Business requirements, the finance side, pricing and packaging, conversion and retention are all stacked on top of it. A lot more variables than most engineering work prepares you for.

Years later, deep into billing and payments work, I've talked to a bunch of other monetization engineers. Almost every one of them has some version of the same origin story. There's no "Monetization 101." You learn it by inheriting a mess and figuring it out in production.

Billing is one of the most important systems a company has, and most people end up owning it by coincidence.
